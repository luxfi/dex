## Tier 1: Standard Market Maker

Entry-level tier for firms beginning their LX DEX market making journey.

### Requirements

| Metric | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Monthly Maker Volume | $10,000,000+ |
| Markets Quoted | 5+ pairs |
| Uptime | 85%+ during trading hours |
| Max Spread | 10 basis points |
| Quote Depth | $50,000 per side |
| Min Capital | $500,000 |

**Quoting Obligations:**
- Provide two-sided quotes 85% of trading hours
- Maximum spread of 10 bps for assigned markets
- Minimum $50K depth at best bid/offer

**Reporting:**

- Annual KYB refresh

## Tier Evaluation

### Monthly Assessment

Tiers are evaluated on the first business day of each month based on:

```python
# Tier Score Calculation
tier_score = (
    volume_score * 0.30 +      # 30% weight
    uptime_score * 0.25 +      # 25% weight
    spread_score * 0.20 +      # 20% weight
    depth_score * 0.15 +       # 15% weight
    market_count_score * 0.10  # 10% weight
)

# Tier Assignment
if tier_score >= 95: tier = 4
elif tier_score >= 85: tier = 3
elif tier_score >= 70: tier = 2
else: tier = 1
```

### Grace Periods

| Transition | Grace Period |
|------------|--------------|
| Upgrade | Immediate |
| Downgrade (1 tier) | 1 month warning |
| Downgrade (2+ tiers) | Immediate |
| New MM | 3 month ramp-up |

### Appeals Process

Market makers may appeal tier decisions within 5 business days by:
1. Submitting written appeal to mm@lux.network
2. Providing supporting documentation
3. Scheduling review call with MM Relations
